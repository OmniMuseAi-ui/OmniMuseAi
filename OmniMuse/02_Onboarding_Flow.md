# OmniMuse Onboarding Flow

## Step 1: Identity Setup
- Name your OmniMuse
- Choose interaction role (Coach, Muse, Strategist, etc.)

## Step 2: Personality Calibration
Sliders + presets:
- Direct ↔ Gentle
- Analytical ↔ Emotional
- Minimal ↔ Expressive

## Step 3: Belief & Meaning Layer (Optional)
- Choose spiritual/religious alignment
- Or select Secular / Neutral

## Step 4: Voice & Presence
- Voice tone
- Verbosity
- Emotional warmth

## Step 5: Visual Space
- Upload your own background
- Or select from 20 curated environments
- Choose font, spacing, motion

## Step 6: Activation
Message appears:
> “OmniMuse is now calibrated to you. You can change anything at any time by asking.”

Button:
[Enter OmniMuse]
